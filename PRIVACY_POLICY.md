# Privacy Policy for StudyBuddy

**Last updated:** August 18, 2026

StudyBuddy ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use our mobile application **StudyBuddy**.

## Information Collection and Use
StudyBuddy is designed to operate primarily on your device. 

- **Local Storage:** Your study schedules, task lists, flashcards, and timers are stored locally on your mobile device.
- **Personal Data:** We do not collect, harvest, or sell personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) through the app.

## Third-Party Services
The app may utilize standard third-party services (such as Google Play Services) that may collect information used to identify your device for functional or analytical purposes.

## Security
We value your trust in using our app. Since your data remains on your local device, protecting your personal study information is tied directly to your device's security settings.

## Children's Privacy
StudyBuddy does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
