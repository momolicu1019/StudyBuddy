Smart Review — make study stick

Top opportunities

    Spaced repetition + due queue

  • High
    Daily goal & streak (data already exists)

    daily_goal_minutes sits in settings with almost no UI. Surface a Today plan and streak on Home.

  • Med
    Weak-subject coaching

    Quiz misses and unmastered cards can drive a short “needs work” strip — then jump into a focused deck.

  • Med
    Deadlines + Focus polish

    Deadlines are buried; Pomodoro promises a break but only runs focus. Promote deadlines and finish the timer loop.

  • Later
    Real sync & docs cleanup

    Email auth is local/demo; FastAPI is unused; README still describes Google Drive. Align product story with code.
